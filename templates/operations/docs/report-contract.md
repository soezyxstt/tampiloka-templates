# Report integration contract (KF29–32/38)

`report(state,userId,URLSearchParams)` reads only the permission-filtered `snapshot`. It returns a detached report; changing either the source state or returned objects cannot change the other. No source state lookups bypass snapshot visibility. Current user permissions always apply, including historical queries.

Filters: `locationId`, `city`, `productId`, `from`, `to`, `basis=recorded|occurred`, `status`, optional `revision`. Empty selectors mean all visible records. Unknown/out-of-scope location IDs are rejected without revealing existence. City and location intersect. Dates normalize to UTC instants and endpoints are inclusive. Invalid basis/date/range is rejected. No implicit timezone/day expansion is performed: callers must supply complete desired instants.

`revision=N` requires the source snapshot to have exactly N; otherwise `REPORT_REVISION` asks the user to refresh. To export exactly the screen, retain its entire report object and pass that to `csvReport(report, displayedRevision)`. The second argument validates the revision. A server GET with the screen's query plus `revision=N&format=csv` must fail if the installation advanced. The parent owns Worker/UI integration; it must pass revision and retain the displayed filters. An omitted revision is explicitly a fresh report request, not a guarantee of screen equality.

## Time and status

- Event sections (`sales`, `refunds`, `unattributedRefunds`, `payments`, `unattributedPayments`, `mutations`, `cashEntries`, `documents`) use `[from,to]`. Occurred basis uses `occurredAt` where available, otherwise `recordedAt`; payment/refund/cash have only recording timestamps.
- Balances are cumulative through `to`, ignoring `from`. Sellable, held and negative flags are recomputed from visible ledger evidence. Transit is outstanding outbound shipment stock at its source location, using transfer-ID transit ledger entries and the same cutoff; it is not a sale or a second destination balance.
- Recorded basis answers what was known by `to`: later-posted backdated mutations are excluded. Occurred basis uses current revision knowledge grouped by occurrence time, including late postings; mutations expose `late`. It is not a time machine that excludes subsequently learned facts.
- Payment status and sale allocation use recorded knowledge through `to` in recorded mode. Occurred mode uses current revision knowledge; `statusBasis` and `statusCutoff` make this explicit. Unknown legacy status before the first history entry is conservatively pending. Original payment `amount` is tendered money, not net retained receipt.
- The model does not store full historical workflow projections. Transfers/counts/shifts and exceptions carry `temporalKind:'current_metadata'`, `asOfRevision`, `asOf`. Their statuses, findings, counters and closure figures are CURRENT, even in an old-period report. Never label them “as of historical cutoff”. Transfers are filtered by creation time, shifts by opening time, counts by current count cutoff. Exceptions are current actionable metadata, not period events. Use dated documents/mutations for historical activity. A shift opened outside the interval is omitted even if closed inside it; cash entries are independently filtered by entry time and shift location.
- A `status` value filters each section by that section's explicit status vocabulary. Sales use `completed`, refunds `refunded`, mutations/cash `posted`, payments actual payment status, shifts `open|closed`, balances `negative|nonnegative`, transfers/counts native workflow status, exceptions exception type. Documents with a status filter must reference a matching row. This is a cross-section filter, not an implied universal workflow status.

## SKU and money

`sales[].quote` always remains the entire original receipt, including original basket totals, promo/profile evidence and line order. Render `sale.selection.lines` and its `gross,discount,net,taxBase,tax,service,serviceTax,total` for SKU-filtered summaries. `totals` aggregates only selected lines. Original receipt detail can still show the full receipt explicitly. Transfers likewise retain originals and expose `selection.lines`.

Payment `selection.amount` is the selected original sale-line bill amount, or the entire unallocated payment when no SKU is selected. Cash change does not count as retained receipts. All selected sale lines partition a paid receipt exactly, without duplicating basket totals. Payments allocated only after a recorded cutoff remain unallocated at that cutoff.

Refund `selection.lines` preserves original line indexes, and `selection.amount` sums matching return-line allocations under a SKU filter. Without a SKU filter it is the complete original refund. Money-only refunds and residual money not assigned to returned lines appear in `unattributedRefunds` with `scope:'location_time_without_sku'`; they remain visible for every SKU but MUST be labeled as unassigned, not counted as that SKU's refund. Unallocated payments similarly appear in `unattributedPayments`. `totals.unattributedRefund`, `unattributedConfirmedReceipts`, `unattributedReviewReceipts` are explicit non-additive disclosure totals. With no SKU, these amounts are already included in the corresponding all-payment/refund totals. Under a SKU filter they are separate and excluded from SKU net receipts. A payment with an inaccessible sale is treated as unattributed; never read hidden receipts to allocate it.

`totals.netReceipts = confirmedReceipts - refund`. Revenue/bill follows sale event time; cash receipts follow payment recording time and refunds refund recording time, so they need not reconcile within a short interval. Goods tax, service fee, service tax, tax base, discounts and goods net remain distinct. Internal transfers never contribute sales. Zero-price lines are retained even when selected money is zero.

Shifts and cash entries have no SKU attribution and are empty under SKU selection. Counts and stock rows filter directly by SKU. Documents filter by explicit SKU in data or a source/reference to matching rows. Unattributed documents are omitted under SKU selection. Documents spanning locations outside the selected/authorized set are conservatively omitted entirely; report cross-links can therefore lack a document body. Parent document lookup must retain its own authorization. Reports expose no unrestricted document search.

## CSV

CSV serializes the supplied report, never rereads mutable state. It records revision, generated timestamp, basis, all filters and temporal contract, totals, balances, mutations, transfers, sales, refunds, unattributed refunds/payments, payments, shifts, cash entries, counts, documents and exceptions. `saleLines` explicitly exports selected gross/discount/net/tax base/goods tax/service/service tax/total. Original receipts and selected-line evidence are nested JSON columns as well. Empty sections retain section markers. Record columns use the union of fields, so later rows cannot silently lose optional fields absent from the first row.

Strings that can start spreadsheet formulas (including leading whitespace) receive an apostrophe; JSON fields are quoted and escaped. Numeric values remain numeric, including negative quantities, cash and variance. UTF-8 BOM and CRLF are included.

## Verification and remaining limits

Executed only: `node --max-old-space-size=64 --experimental-transform-types --test --test-concurrency=1 templates/operations/tests/report-contract.test.mjs`. Seven focused regression tests passed (single process, under 0.4 seconds). Coverage includes mixed SKU basket receipts/refunds, unassigned refund disclosure, late recorded versus occurred stock, recomputed negatives, later payment resolution/allocation, current workflow labels, location/city cash scope, permissions, internal transfers and historical outbound transit, CSV section completeness/formula protection and stale revision rejection.

No install, builds, typechecks, browsers, other-family suites or heavy checks were run, as requested. This is functional report evidence only; UI/Worker wiring, runtime performance, full historical workflow reconstruction, indexed/paged exports, monthly availability, backup/recovery objectives and production acceptance are not proven. Metadata is current even when its underlying creation falls within a historical window; complete past workflow replay requires additional domain evidence and is not claimed here.
